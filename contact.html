Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the fastest way to modify my reservation?

You can instantly reschedule or update your booking time slot through your Player Dashboard up to 4 hours before your session starts.

Q: Do you offer parking on-site?

Yes, free secure underground parking is available for all LaserX Arena guests and tournament participants for up to 3 hours.

Q: Can we bring our own food for private parties?

Birthday cakes and specialized dietary items are welcome in private party rooms. Full catering packages can also be arranged through our events desk.
