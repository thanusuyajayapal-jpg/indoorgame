What is the minimum age requirement to play?

+
Players must be at least 7 years old to comfortably carry our lightweight infrared phasers and safely navigate the multi-level blacklight maze.
02 //

Do I need to book in advance?

+
While walk-ins are welcome based on live arena slot availability, we strongly recommend booking online—especially on weekends and evenings—to guarantee your squad's preferred start time.
03 //

What should I wear to the arena?

+
Wear comfortable athletic clothing and closed-toe sneakers with good grip. Dark clothing helps you blend into the shadows, while white or neon accents glow under the UV blacklights.
04 //

Can we host private birthday parties or corporate events?

+
Yes—private arena buyouts include dedicated game marshals, custom team tournament brackets, live scoreboard replays, and access to our VIP briefing lounge.
