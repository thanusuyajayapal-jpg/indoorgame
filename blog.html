Leaderboard & Rating FAQs

Q: How are ranking scores calculated?

Scores are calculated using a blend of match wins, kill-to-death ratios, objective captures, and weapon accuracy percentages across all rated matches.

Q: When does the global leaderboard reset?

Leaderboards reset at the end of each calendar quarter, with top-ranking operatives receiving permanent legacy hall-of-fame badges.

Q: How do I register my clan name?

You can link your squad profile and register your custom clan tag directly through your personal Operator Player Dashboard.
